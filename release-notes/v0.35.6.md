## Wrathmaker v0.35.6

**Crafting is back**
- Fixed an issue that prevented the Workbench from opening.
- Added a material-effects readout that updates when you change material or tier. See weapon attack bonuses, armour bonuses, shield durability and relevant Voidborn effects before crafting.
- Moved this readout beneath Total Materials Required for a more compact layout.

**Omnipotentium**
- Renamed the material family from Omnipotassium to Omnipotentium. The six tier names and existing crafted items remain compatible.
- These exceptionally rare ingots are awarded only by the GM and cannot be gathered.
- Ingots have no standard market or sale value, and add no material-price premium to crafted equipment. Older ingot prices are cleared when the GM loads the world.

**Tidier compendiums**
- All six Omni tiers now have a dedicated **Omnipotisium Ingots** compendium inside **Pathfinder 2E Wrathmaker**.
- **Void Protection** also has its own compendium. Its stock Items-tab reference is backed up and removed once the compendium is available; effects already on creatures remain in place.
- Omni ingot references are no longer automatically added to the Items tab. Existing inventory and world ingot copies are preserved.

Update through Foundry or Forge, restart the world, and refresh your browser.
