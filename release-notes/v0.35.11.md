## Wrathmaker v0.35.11 — Faster Crafting & Masterstroke Actions

**Crafting improvements**
- New projects take 20% fewer base crafting days, rounded up to a minimum of one day. Existing projects keep their recorded duration and progress.
- Only the lead artisan's owner or a GM can roll Work Blocks. Creating a project no longer grants permission to roll for somebody else.

**Masterstrokes on your character sheet**
- Equipped Masterstroke items now grant abilities in the Actions tab.
- Chosen abilities such as Artisan's Triumph must be armed before their eligible roll. Passive benefits remain automatic.
- Arming does not spend the use: the effect spends it only when it applies.
- Daily, round and turn abilities use native counters. Daily uses also reset with Rest for the Night; encounter abilities use the encounter tracker.
- Spent abilities stay visible. Unequipping removes the granted entry without restoring spent uses.
- All 16 current Masterstrokes share consistent descriptions, requirements, effects and availability, with free-action icons for chosen abilities.

**Cleaner item and chat descriptions**
- Crafted items show their maker, installed property runes, material benefits and Masterstroke beneath the description and in expanded inventory entries.
- Added material and Masterstroke flavour, a native decorative border and matching grey background.
- Crafting chat reports are compact, use normal text colours, and omit repeated progress totals and the extra DC readout. PF2e's native roll message retains the result display.
- Fixed a repeated sheet-refresh loop that could cause flashing controls and missed clicks.
- Updated the player and GM guides.

After updating, fully reload Foundry for every connected user. Equipped Masterstroke actions update automatically while an active GM is connected. Activate chosen abilities before rolling; they no longer activate automatically without being armed.
