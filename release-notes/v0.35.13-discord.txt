**Wrathmaker v0.35.13 — updated 25 October**

**Gate Attenuator hotfix — 25 October**
- Six craftable tiers grant **+0 / +1 / +2 / +3 / +4 / +5** to impulse attacks, including Elemental Blast, and impulse DCs while worn.
- Enchanting leads; craft with 2 Mana Gem units and 1 metal or wood frame unit. Blacksmithing or Carpentry supports the frame.
- Only the strongest worn attenuator applies. Item bonuses do not stack. **Tier 6 has no Constitution or apex benefit.**
- Reload and use **Refresh All** to add the entries to **Wrathmaker Crafting Items**.

**Crafting changes**
- Work Block DC = **10 × project tier**. Gathering and enchanting are unchanged.
- Best applicable tools grant **+5 × the tool's tier**: Tier 3 tools give +15. GMs configure tools on their Details tab.
- New projects take one-third less base time, rounded up, minimum 1 day. Existing projects keep their recorded duration.
- GMs can override project DCs; Workbench DC readouts are GM-only.
- Critical successes pause for manual Edge/Masterstroke table rolls. Spend 1 Hero Point manually to reroll either table and **choose either result**.
- The lead crafter's owner or GM confirms results with **Complete Craft**. Opening tables preserves the results window and selections.
- GMs can edit an existing item's Masterstroke on its Details tab; changing it resets limited uses.

**Nephilim Bonds**
- Each shared party point unlocks one bond slot per member. Repeat choices stack; point loss suspends excess choices.
- Five bonds unlock at 2 points: Communion, Pincer, Charge, Valor and Stamina. Ally bonds have no distance limit.
- Charge: move 20 feet before the next melee Strike that turn, then enable the +3 attack toggle.
- Stamina adds one prepared/spontaneous slot per accessible rank per selection.

**Same version number:** reinstall if already on v0.35.13 to get the hotfix.
Playtest rules remain subject to change.
