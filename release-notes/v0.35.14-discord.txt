**Wrathmaker v0.35.14**

**Custom Ores & Omnipotentium**
- Omnipotentium is now **one Tier 7 ingot**, replacing the six previous Omni tiers.
- Defaults to **+6 material bonus and +1 extra weapon damage die**. That die adds alongside **Striking**, without replacing it.
- Find it in **Custom Ores**. GMs can add new ores, edit bonuses and extra dice, and create ingot references from the Control Panel.
- **Voidborn** is an automated property; other tags are descriptive. Custom ores are GM-awarded, cannot be gathered and have no standard sale price.
- Tier 7 crafting uses **DC 70**, with normal supporting materials capped at Tier 6.

**Nephilim Bonds**
- Improved matching-ally detection for **Ao's Communion, Ao's Pincer and Valor of Ao**.
- New **Bond Aura** effects show whether a matching ally is available. These are party-wide, with **no distance limit**.
- Roll modifiers now show just the gift's name.

**Visual fixes**
- More readable Custom Ores and material-editor headings.

**GM update step:** restart the world and run **Refresh All**. Old Omnipotentium items move to Tier 7 while quantities and Striking runes are preserved.

*Playtest rules and pricing remain subject to change based on feedback and gameplay.*
