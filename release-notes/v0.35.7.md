## Wrathmaker v0.35.7 — Professions & Enchanting

**Simpler professions**
- Choose one profession and retrain it whenever needed. Additional Lore progresses normally, and the +2 profession bonus remains.
- Extra profession choices at later levels are removed. Specializations are optional and off by default.
- Dragon-scale reinforcement uses Leatherwork under the default rules.

**Enchant during or after crafting**
- Add an enchanter to the crafting team and use **Enchant Item** to choose property runes for the finished item.
- Each rune costs **one Mana Gem**. When crafting, gems are reserved with the project and consumed on completion.
- Mana Gem tiers unlock rune levels up to **1, 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20**.
- Material bonuses supply property-rune slots, capped at **three**. No potency rune is required.
- Finished weapons and armour can still be enchanted from the Workbench's **Enchant** button.
- Browse eligible runes with descriptions and bookmarks opening their full details.

**Better teamwork**
- Each additional artisan adds **+1 to crafting rolls**, up to **+5 with six artisans**.
- This stacks with the profession bonus and keeps the existing teamwork time reduction, helping you reach Crafting Edges and Masterstrokes.

**Cleaner sheets and guides**
- Planned runes appear beside the material description in an aligned, scrolling yellow panel.
- Nephilim Bonds use bold names and a three-dot details button matching other character fields.
- New GM custom items go into **GM crafted items**, with player access disabled.
- Player and GM guides cover the current rules without retired specialization and Mark chapters.

After updating, restart the world and refresh your browser. The compendium guides refresh when a GM loads the world; imported journal copies remain unchanged.
