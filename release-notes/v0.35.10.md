## Wrathmaker v0.35.10 — Crafting Enchantment Controls

- Assigned Enchanting artisans now have an explicit **Enchant Item** button in the crafting screen for GMs and players, including before equipment is dropped in.
- Added **Enchant Crafted Item** at the top when planning a weapon or armour, making it easier to add property runes before completing a project.
- Renamed the separate inventory action to **Enchant Existing Item**.
- Improved messages explaining missing artisans, eligible equipment, or Mana Gems.

After updating the hosted module, reload Foundry. Assign an Enchanting artisan in Craft, choose your weapon or armour, and use **Enchant Item** below that artisan. Normal material rune slots and Mana Gem costs still apply. No Refresh All migration is required.
