## Wrathmaker v0.35.9 — Striking Rune Fix

**Materials and Striking now appear together**
- Fixed the weapon sheet hiding the Striking rune selector when a crafting material was selected.
- Crafted Material and the native Striking Rune selector now appear separately, so you can select or change Striking normally.
- Materials still improve attack rolls. Striking runes supply extra base weapon damage dice.
- Existing Striking rune selections are preserved.

After updating, refresh Foundry and reopen the weapon sheet. No Refresh All migration is needed for this display fix.
