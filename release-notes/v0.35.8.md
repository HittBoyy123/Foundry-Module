## Wrathmaker v0.35.8 — Masterstrokes & Refresh All

**Masterstrokes just got stronger**
- New weapon and equipment tables grant powerful, automatically applied benefits: improved attacks, defensive protection, extra durability, lighter equipment and more.
- Masterstrokes still require a critical success on a finishing Work Block, followed by Masterstroke Opportunity and enough work to complete the item.
- Incompatible results reroll automatically. Each item keeps **one Masterstroke**, including through upgrades.
- Limited uses are tracked automatically and stay with the item when transferred. Overwhelming Impact lets you choose to knock the target prone or push it up to 10 feet.
- Limitless Channel grants an extra property-rune slot, allowing **four total**. The extra rune must still be acquired and applied.

**Profession feat restored**
- Your chosen profession grants **Specialty Crafting**, alongside Additional Lore and the existing **+2 profession bonus**.
- Specialty Crafting increases with normal Crafting proficiency. Circumstance bonuses do not stack.

**GM Refresh All**
- A new settings button updates module-managed items, professions, projects, guides and rollable tables, and removes retired module effects.
- A private backup is saved before changes. The completion pop-out lists what was added, removed, changed, skipped or failed.
- Existing Masterstrokes migrate to the new tables. The first result is retained when compatible; otherwise the first compatible result is used. The report records the change.
- Items reserved for upgrades are skipped until the upgrade is completed or cancelled.

**After updating:** restart Foundry, refresh your browser, then the GM can use **Module Settings → Wrathmaker → Refresh All**. Backups and saved reports appear in the GM-only **Wrathmaker Backups** journal folder.

Validation: 416 automated tests passed. The new combat automation still needs live playtesting.
