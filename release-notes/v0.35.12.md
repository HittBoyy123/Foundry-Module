**Wrathmaker v0.35.12**

**Batch crafting**
- Craft **1–4 identical potions, oils, elixirs or alchemical bombs** together. Materials multiply with quantity; base work stays the same as one item.
- Craft arrows, bolts, firearm rounds, sling ammunition and blowgun darts in **batches of 10**, up to **40 pieces** per project.
- Mundane ammunition uses **Tier 1** resources. Magical ammunition and consumable variants require materials matching their item level.

**Spell scrolls**
- **Bookmaking** leads scroll crafting; **Enchanting** fills the optional support role.
- Someone involved must know the spell. No prepared spell or available spell slot is spent.
- Browse a searchable spellbook with spell descriptions and material tiers. Use **+** to choose the scroll rank.
- Scroll rank is capped by the **highest spell rank available among participating casters**. Cantrips, focus spells and rituals are excluded.
- Each scroll costs **1 Wood + 1 Mana Gem** at the selected tier. Craft **1–4 identical scrolls** together.

**Teamwork**
- Filling the lead and all named support roles unlocks **25% faster work**. A generic assistant alone does not unlock this benefit.
- Additional artisans still add **+1 to Work Block rolls**, up to **+5**.

**Try the professions**
- GMs can create **11 level-5 example characters**, one per profession, with a mix of spellcasters and non-spellcasters.
- Use **Create Profession Example Characters** in **Wrathmaker Demo Tools**. The characters are also saved in a reusable world compendium.

**After updating**
- Restart the world and refresh your browser. Use **Refresh All** and review its report.
- These are playtest rules: rules and prices may change with player feedback.
