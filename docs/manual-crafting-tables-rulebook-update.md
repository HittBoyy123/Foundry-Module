# Crafting Edges, Masterstrokes and Hero Points

Replace instructions saying the Workbench automatically rolls and applies Crafting Edges or Masterstrokes with the following:

On a critical success, the Workbench saves the Work Block and pauses before applying progress. Roll the **Crafting Edge** table: use d3 for an ordinary block, or the **Final Block (d4)** button if accelerated work could finish the contribution. The Workbench tells you which die to use.

You may spend **1 Hero Point** to reroll either the Crafting Edge or Masterstroke table and **choose either result**. Deduct the Hero Point from the lead crafter's character sheet before rerolling. Table rolls and Hero Point spending are handled manually.

For a qualifying Masterstroke Opportunity that completes the contribution at 100% work, roll the appropriate **Masterstroke — Weapons** or **Masterstroke — Equipment** table. Reroll incompatible results. Items retain the existing limit of one Masterstroke, including through upgrades. Consumables and ammunition do not gain Masterstrokes.

The lead crafter's owner or a GM opens **Workbench → Projects → Resolve Crafting Tables**, selects the final Edge and, if eligible, the final Masterstroke, and clicks **Apply Final Results**. Select the reserved material for a Material Conservation result. Closing the window or opening a table leaves the work pending, so you can return later without rerolling the crafting check.

Confirmation applies the progress, conservation credit or next-block bonus once. A selected Masterstroke is attached to the finished item when crafting is completed. Its existing passive effects and usable abilities continue to function normally.
