# Profession example characters

Run **Create Profession Example Characters** from **Wrathmaker Demo Tools** as a GM. The first run creates eleven level-5 player characters in **Wrathmaker - Profession Examples**, plus a locked world Actor compendium called **Wrathmaker Profession Examples**. Drag a compendium entry into Actors for a fresh copy. In the local preview, the same setup macro is also on hotbar slot 1.

The builder reads the installed PF2e Iconics pack. It keeps the native class, ancestry, equipment, spells and artwork references, adds the selected Wrathmaker profession and its normal grants, and provides at least trained Crafting for demonstrations. It does not bundle copies of Paizo actor data or artwork in the module. These are profession-adapted demo builds, not new official iconics.

## Testing ideas

- **Consumables:** Fumbus as Alchemy lead, Seoni as Glassmaking support.
- **Scrolls:** Feiya as Bookmaking lead, Ezren as Enchanting support. Select a spell known by either, or involve another spellcaster.
- **Weapons:** Valeros as lead, with the required material support profession.
- **Teamwork:** compare a solo lead, all named support roles, and generic assistants.
- **Permissions:** assign a player Owner access to their test character. Observer access only lets them inspect it; only the lead character's owner or a GM may roll a Work Block.

Pottery, Weaving and Tailoring can assist but do not yet have dedicated recipes. No materials or party membership are automatically added. Existing characters and saved templates are preserved when rerunning setup; it does not reset spent resources or overwrite your edits.

The generated Actor compendium belongs to the current world. Running the module's setup macro in another world generates a new local set from that world's installed PF2e system.
