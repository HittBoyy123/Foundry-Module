# Crafting time amendment

Reduce the previous base crafting duration by one third, round up to a whole day, and use a minimum of one day: **new base days = max(1, ceiling(previous base days × 2 ÷ 3))**. This is an additional reduction after the earlier 20% adjustment.

Examples: 3 days become 2; 4 become 3; 6 become 4; 8 become 6. One-day and two-day projects retain their duration because of the minimum and rounding.

This applies to newly planned projects. Existing projects keep their recorded required progress and completed work. Material costs, work-block outcomes and teamwork bonuses are unchanged.
