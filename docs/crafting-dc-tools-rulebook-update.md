# Crafting DCs and tools — pending next release

Crafting Work Blocks use a base DC of **10 × material tier**: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 or 60. Recipe difficulty adjustments still apply. A GM custom DC replaces the calculated result. Checks already saved awaiting table results keep their recorded DC.

Gathering and enchanting are unchanged by this amendment.

The lead crafter gains **+5 per tier** from their best applicable carried tool set, from +5 at Tier 1 to +30 at Tier 6. Tool sets do not stack. This is an item bonus and follows normal item-bonus stacking. The set must match the lead crafter's relevant profession. Dropped items and zero-quantity sets do not qualify.

To configure a set, a GM or assistant GM opens an equipment item's **Details → Crafting Tools — GM**, selects its profession and tier, then clicks **Save Tool Set**. Put the item in the lead crafter's inventory. Existing unconfigured equipment does not automatically grant a tiered bonus; its name alone does not establish its tier or profession. Select **Not a crafting tool set** to remove this configuration.
