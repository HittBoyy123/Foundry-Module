# Pending Nephilim bond rulebook update

Replace level-threshold and distinct-choice rules with:

Each shared party Nephilim point permits one bond per character. Selecting a bond does not spend a point. Repeat choices stack: two Nephilim Skin choices grant +4 AC. Ao’s Will and Nephilim Form can repeat the same save or attribute or select different ones.

Slots activate in numerical order. If points fall from three to two, slot three becomes inactive; its selection remains saved and returns automatically when the party reaches three points again. A character with no party has no active bond allowance. For membership in multiple parties, use the highest single party total without adding pools together.

Existing level-5, 10, 15 and 20 choices map to slots 1, 2, 3 and 4. Character level no longer unlocks bonds, though Ao’s Toughness still grants its listed HP per character level. The existing shared tracker remains capped at ten points.

## Bonds available from two shared points

These consume normal bond selections, rather than granting extra selections. All become inactive below two points, including a choice saved in slot one.

| Bond | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Ao's Communion | +3 AC if another party member has an active copy of this bond. |
| Ao's Pincer | +3 to attack rolls if another party member has an active copy of this bond. |
| Nephilim Charge | After moving at least 20 feet immediately before the next melee Strike on the same turn, gain +3 to that attack roll. |
| Valor of Ao | +4 to a saving throw of your choice if another party member has an active copy of this bond. Each character chooses their own saving throw. |
| Ao's Stamina | One extra spell slot at every accessible rank, per selection. |

Ally bonds work anywhere, without a distance limit. Repeating your own bond stacks its bonus, but the number of qualifying allies does not multiply it.

**Foundry use:** Charge is a player-confirmed roll option: enable it after moving the required distance. It automatically clears after the melee attack or a combat turn change. Movement itself is not measured automatically.

Stamina adds native prepared or spontaneous slots, so normal preparation, casting and resting apply. Cantrips, focus spells and innate spell uses do not receive slots. With multiple spellcasting entries, the first eligible entry receives each rank's extra slot; the benefit is not duplicated across entries. Point loss removes the added capacity without removing ordinary slots.
